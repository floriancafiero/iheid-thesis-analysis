# Multilingual, hierarchical entity normalization

Run `python analysis/entity_hierarchy.py entities_combined.jsonl.zip` (Python standard library only). Input is supplied locally, **not** added to Git. Exports in `analysis/hierarchy_output/`: normalized document-entity counts, institution/corpus summaries, organization families/continents, matched variants, and unresolved top 500 NER strings. Exact aliases are merged across French/English and several NER labels. Selected cities/regions (Shanghai→China, Vaud→Switzerland) *inherit* their country without being treated as synonymous with it. Historical states (USSR, Yugoslavia where included) must not silently merge into successors; contested territories intentionally remain unresolved. UNICEF, UNHCR etc. are distinct entities, whose documents are aggregated without duplicate counting under the UN-system family.

This intentionally conservative alias vocabulary is **not an exhaustive Wikidata linker**. Many raw NER strings are OCR noise. Do not treat inferred geographic presence as research focus: Paris/New York/Geneva/London and university towns often occur in bibliographies. Compare direct and expanded counts, and calibrate document-level salience separately. Raw JSONL has no year/degree metadata, so chronological claims need a verified doc_id→year/degree join. Unrecognized ID prefixes remain Unknown. The accompanying sandbox export from the initial analysis uses a broader exploratory alias dictionary and is *not* bit-for-bit identical to this compact version. Both preserve the direct/inherited distinction.
