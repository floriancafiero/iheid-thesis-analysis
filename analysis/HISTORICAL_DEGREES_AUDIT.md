# Historical degrees: missing metadata evidence

The NER corpus contains **762 dated items from 1927–1989**, all labeled `Master/Other`. That label conflates master's work, diplomas, and unclassified records and **does not prove they are masters**. Neither does an extensive text prove PhD status.

Two external source checks establish: (a) Nathan Feinberg's 1930 *La juridiction de la Cour permanente de justice internationale dans le système des mandats* is a diploma memoir, not a PhD (CiNii NCID BA33627444); (b) Harry Joiner's 1969 *A Theoretical Model of International Negotiation* is expressly called a Ph.D. dissertation in a specialized bibliography (entry 448, [source](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780429702044_A36690995/preview-9780429702044_A36690995.pdf)). Both are mislumped into `Master/Other`. As a result, 760 historical records remain unverified. The conservative doctoral subset increases from 1,264 to **1,265**, but **cannot support a century-scale doctoral trend**. Do not plot those 760 as doctorates, nor as confirmed masters.

Run `python analysis/audit_historical_degrees.py thesis_metadata.csv entities_combined.jsonl.zip` to export the historical classification audit and an updated conservative PhD JSONL. Evidence-based overrides are kept in `analysis/historical_degree_evidence.csv`. The underlying metadata and NER are untouched.

For a proper historical reclassification, retrieve the original ALTO catalogue source `IHEID_LOT_1_MD_FILTRE_SUR_THESES.xlsx` referenced by `PIPELINE_DOCUMENTATION.md`; verify columns such as `Directeur(s)` and degree/qualification and join by original bibliographic ID. This original catalogue is **not** among the user's current uploads or this GitHub repository. Presence of a supervisor is not alone a universal definition of PhD. Until that audit is complete, previous findings beginning in 1990 remain limited to the labelled doctoral subset and cannot describe the full founding-to-present century.
