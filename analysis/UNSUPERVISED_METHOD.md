# Statistically calibrated document salience (no annotations)

Run `pip install numpy scipy scikit-learn` and `python analysis/unsupervised_salience.py entities_combined.jsonl.zip`. The input ZIP is supplied locally and is not committed.

**Purpose.** Replace the arbitrary minimum-count and per-10k-word thresholds with an entity-specific, data-driven classification. For each named country/organization, the script first fits an overdispersed gamma–Poisson model to raw counts with an offset for processed word count. Its empirical-Bayes posterior mean rate shrinks noisy very-short documents toward the corpus baseline. Among documents with at least one mention, it then fits one- and two-component Gaussian mixtures to log rates. The mixture with a high-frequency regime is retained **only when** BIC improves by ≥10 and the estimated two regime rates differ by ≥1.5×. The primary indicator uses posterior probability ≥0.90 of the high-frequency regime; 0.80 and 0.95 sensitivity outputs are provided.

**Interpretation:** this is *unusually concentrated mentions*, not proof that the document's subject is that country. The Gaussian-mixture assumption can be imperfect (citations, OCR, repeated text, historical names); inspect diagnostics and stability. A `not_separable` outcome means the data do not support a meaningful two-regime split for that entity: do not force a prevalence score. For infrequently named entities (<30 positive documents or fewer than four distinct positive counts), no model is fitted. Conservative, incomplete alias dictionaries are inherited from `document_salience.py`; review NER coverage before interpreting absences.

**Historical comparison:** supply `--metadata metadata.csv` with `doc_id,year,institution,degree`. Without that information the output is document-level only; never infer year or diploma from an arbitrary ID. To calculate prevalence by decade/institution, take the percentage of documents with `high_90pct=1` over **all** corpus documents in that group, not merely the documents where the entity occurs. Keep IUED master's and doctoral corpora distinct. The model is fitted on the entire corpus to keep the classification definition consistent over time.
